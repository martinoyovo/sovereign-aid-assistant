# Field Report — Bridge collapse, Northern Reach (incoming)

**Date:** 2026-05-30
**Filed by:** Mobile assessment team, Northern Reach corridor

A footbridge on the main access route into the Northern Reach settlement collapsed
after heavy rain, cutting off roughly forty households from the distribution point.
The team reached the cut-off cluster on foot to carry out a rapid assessment.

## Key findings

- **Protection concern:** three unaccompanied adolescents were found sheltering
  together without an adult caregiver on record. They need a child-protection
  referral and an **urgent follow-up** visit before the next aid cycle.
- Two households include members with chronic conditions who have now missed
  a scheduled medication delivery because the route is impassable.
- Drinking water is being collected from an unprotected source; a water-quality
  check is recommended.

## Recommended actions

1. Prioritise a temporary crossing or an alternative delivery route so the
   cut-off cluster is not skipped in the next distribution.
2. Open a child-protection case for the three adolescents and arrange the
   urgent follow-up visit.
3. Add the chronic-condition households to the next mobile-clinic run.
